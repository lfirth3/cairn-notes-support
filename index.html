Support

Help with Cairn Notes, the notes app for iPhone that remembers where you left each note.

If something isn't working, it helps to say which iPhone you have and which version of iOS it's running. In the app, Settings › Help › Send Feedback fills in the app's version for you.

Common questions

I'm not getting a reminder when I arrive somewhere

Arrival reminders need three things switched on:

  • Location set to “Always”: iPhone Settings › Apps › Cairn Notes › Location.
  • Notifications allowed: iPhone Settings › Apps › Cairn Notes › Notifications.
  • Arrival notifications turned on in the app: Cairns tab › the cog › Notifications.

Also worth knowing:

  • A reminder appears when you arrive, so you need to have been away from the place (about 100 m or more) and come back.
  • A place that has just reminded you stays quiet for an hour.
  • If you chose “Don't Remind Me Here” for a place, turn its reminders back on from that place's ⋯ menu.
  • iOS lets an app watch 20 places at a time. Cairn Notes watches the ones nearest to you and re-picks them as you travel.
There's no Cairn Summary on my notes

The written summary uses Apple Intelligence, on your iPhone. On iPhones that don't support it, or where it's turned off, Cairn Notes shows a simpler summary put together from your notes instead. You can turn summaries on or off, and choose their tone, in the app's Settings.

How do I back up my notes or move to a new iPhone?

Your notes are kept only on your iPhone, and are included in your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer). Restoring that backup on a new iPhone brings them with it. Cairn Notes doesn't sync through iCloud.

For a copy you can keep anywhere, use Settings › Export Notes in the app.

I deleted a note by mistake

An Undo button appears for a few seconds after you delete. After that the note is gone for good. Archiving, on the other hand, never deletes anything: archived notes are under Archived on the Cairns tab.

How do locked notes work?

Locking a note hides it behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Locked notes are kept out of search, summaries, widgets, notifications, sharing and exports. The lock hides the note inside the app; the note is stored on your iPhone like your others, protected by your iPhone's own security.

Apple Health figures aren't showing in the Logbook

Apple Health is optional. Turn it on in the app's Settings, then check what Cairn Notes may read in the Health app under Sharing › Apps. Only figures that Health actually has are shown, and Activity rings need an Apple Watch or a move goal set in the Fitness app.

My trail isn't showing on the Lock Screen

When you start a trail, your next stop, the stops done so far and the sunset time appear on the Lock Screen (and in the Dynamic Island on iPhones that have one). The first time, iOS asks whether to allow Live Activities from Cairn Notes — tap Allow. If you said no, turn them back on in iPhone Settings › Apps › Cairn Notes › Live Activities.

It moves on by itself as you reach each stop, and goes when you finish or end the trail.

How do waymarks and their levels work?

Waymarks are the badges in the Logbook. Most can be earned again and again: each one counts how many times you've done it and goes up a level at milestones (1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100), with a ring filling towards the next. Reach every level and the badge turns gold. Tap a badge to see its levels and when you reached each one.

They're worked out from your own notes and trails, on your iPhone. Deleting a note never takes a level away.

How do I send a postcard of a place?

Press and hold a cairn on the Cairns tab, or tap ⋯ on a place, and choose Send a Postcard. You'll see the picture before it goes: the cairn on a hillside with the place's name, height, grid reference and a line from its notes. Locked notes are never used.

How do I pin a place or sort by distance?

Press and hold a cairn on the Cairns tab and choose Pin to Top; pinned places stay at the top in the order you pinned them. To see the nearest places first, tap the sort and filter button beside the search box and choose Nearest first.

How do I use a trail with Komoot, Garmin or OS Maps?

Plan a trail on the Map tab, tap the share button on the trail panel and choose Export GPX. To bring a route in, open a .gpx file and choose Cairn Notes, or use Import a Route when planning a trail.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the app removes everything it has stored. Nothing is kept anywhere else: there are no accounts and no servers.

Your privacy

Everything you write stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

What you need

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Cairn Notes is free.
