Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Cairn Notes is a notes app for iPhone that remembers where you left each note. It was built to keep your notes private: everything you write stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers run by Cairn Notes, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy explains what the app handles, where it is kept, and the few occasions when information leaves your phone.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The developer never receives your notes, your location, your photos, your voice clips or anything about how you use the app.
  • Your notes are stored only on your iPhone (and in your own iPhone backups, if you use them).
  • A few features use Apple's own services — maps, place names, walking routes and, on some devices, speech recognition. These are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Nothing leaves your phone through Cairn Notes unless you choose to share it.

Your notes are shared with the app's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets so they can show your nearest notes. Widgets never show locked or archived notes.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, your Cairn Notes data is included in that backup, which is handled by Apple and protected by your Apple Account. Cairn Notes does not sync notes through iCloud or any other service.

Your location

Cairn Notes uses your location to tag each note with where you wrote it, to remind you when you arrive somewhere you've left notes, and to show nearby places, trails and the distance to them.

  • Arrival reminders use iOS's built-in region monitoring, which runs on your phone. The app is never sent a continuous record of where you go, and no location history is kept.
  • "Always" location access is only needed for arrival reminders. You can turn reminders off in the app, or change location access at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

When information leaves your phone

Cairn Notes only sends information to Apple services that are built into iOS, and only to make a feature work:

  • Maps, place names and search — to draw the map, name the place where you left a note, and search for places, the relevant location or search text is sent to Apple Maps.
  • Walking and driving routes — when you plan a trail, the positions of its stops are sent to Apple Maps to work out the route.
  • Directions — choosing "Directions" or starting a trail opens the Apple Maps app with that destination.
  • Speech recognition — voice notes are transcribed on your phone wherever your language supports it. On devices or languages where it doesn't, iOS may send the audio to Apple to transcribe it. Voice notes that are transcribed later, in the background, are only ever transcribed on the phone.

Apple handles this information under Apple's Privacy Policy. It is not sent to the developer.

These features run entirely on your iPhone and send nothing anywhere: Cairn Summaries (written by Apple's on-device model), reading Apple Health, reading text in photos, voice note headings, daylight and sunset times, Ordnance Survey grid references, trail ordering, and day plans (including reading times like "before noon" in your notes).

Apple Health

If you turn it on, Cairn Notes reads from Apple Health to show you, in the Logbook, your steps, walking distance, flights climbed, active energy, heart rate, workouts and Activity rings for the trails you walk.

  • It is off until you ask for it, and you choose exactly what it may read in iOS's own Health permission screen. You can change that at any time in the Health app under Sharing › Apps.
  • It is read-only. Cairn Notes never writes to Apple Health.
  • Health data is read when you open the Logbook and shown on screen. It is not saved by Cairn Notes, not included in exports or anything you share, not sent to the developer or anyone else, and never used for advertising.

Things you choose to share

When you share a note, a place's notes, a trail, a postcard, a GPX file or a trip plan, the content goes to whoever and wherever you pick in the iOS share sheet (for example Messages, Mail, AirDrop or another app). Cairn Notes doesn't keep a copy or see where it was sent. Locked notes are never included in anything shared or exported.

Locked notes and Face ID

You can lock notes behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Authentication is handled by iOS; Cairn Notes never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode. Locked notes are kept out of search, summaries, widgets, notifications, sharing and exports. Note that locking hides a note inside the app; it is stored on your phone in the same way as your other notes, protected by your iPhone's own security.

Notifications

Arrival reminders and trip-plan check-ins are local notifications, created on your phone. No notification content passes through any server.

Permissions

Cairn Notes asks for each permission only when a feature needs it: location, notifications, microphone and speech recognition (voice notes), camera and photos (attaching a picture), Face ID (locked notes) and Apple Health (the Logbook, only if you turn it on). You can change any of them at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Add to Calendar on a day plan opens Apple's own new-event screen, already filled in. You choose whether to save it. Cairn Notes is never given access to your calendar and can't see what's in it.

Deleting your data

Delete a note, or a whole place, in the app and it's removed from your phone, along with its photo and voice clip. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Cairn Notes doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date. Any change that affects how your data is handled will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
